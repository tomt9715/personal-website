About

My Story

I have a computer science degree from Mercy University, and computers are still something I love. I tinker, I've picked up design skills along the way, and that curiosity hasn't gone anywhere. But after a few years in corporate roles, I felt trapped. Monday through Friday belonged to someone else, on repeat, for two days off that never felt like enough. Early mornings, long commutes there and back, and by the time I got home there were maybe two or three hours before I had to sleep and do it all again the next day.

Nursing kept coming up, through friends, family, my wife. Not as an easy path, but as a genuinely rewarding one: hard work, but work that matters, centered on real care for someone during their hour of need. That struck a chord. I've always considered myself an outgoing person, and looking back at the jobs I've had, including sales roles where I spent my days talking with and convincing people, the part I actually loved was never the task itself. It was the conversation.

So I made the switch. I'm finishing my ABSN at Manhattanville University now, on track to graduate this December and sit for my NCLEX in early 2027. The plan from here is straightforward: pass the boards, start working as an RN, and build from there.
